Against Idle Land

A case for a self-assessed property tax, to curb dereliction and limit monopoly pricing.

Scattered across Dublin city centre is a blight of derelict buildings. These are an eyesore, a drag on the economy, and by limiting supply they contribute to higher commercial and residential rents. The value of these properties is tied to the surrounding neighbourhood, whose value in turn depends on the investment of private individuals and the state.
