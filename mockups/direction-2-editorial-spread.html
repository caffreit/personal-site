Landscape · Swiss Alps

Alpine Dawn

Limited Edition — 7 of 25

The Story

Standing at the ridge before sunrise, with the valley still wrapped in indigo, there is a particular silence that belongs only to altitude. The air is thin enough that you hear your own heartbeat. Then the sun arrives — not gradually, but with sudden conviction — and the peaks ignite one by one like a chain of signal fires lit along the spine of the continent.

This photograph was made in that narrow corridor of time between the first and second light. The exposure lasted eight seconds, which is exactly how long the warm tones clung to the eastern face before the whole scene shifted to cool, diffuse morning. It is a document of a moment that existed for less time than it takes to describe.

"A photograph is not made by the person who presses the shutter — it is made by the person who decides to stand in a particular place and wait."

Printed on Hahnemühle Photo Rag, each edition is hand-inspected and signed. The cotton-rag surface absorbs pigment with a softness that feels closer to watercolour than photography — appropriate for an image that is, at its core, about the quality of light.
