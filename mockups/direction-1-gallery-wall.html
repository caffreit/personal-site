Story behind the shot

Standing alone at 3,200 metres, the world below had dissolved into white. There was nothing but silence and the intermittent crack of ice settling in the early light. I waited forty minutes for the peaks to emerge — and when they did, it lasted only seconds before the cloud swallowed them again.

This image is from that brief window. A reminder that the most extraordinary moments are often the most fleeting.
