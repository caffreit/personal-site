Irish tax lab redesign

Editorial directions

Aesthetic-only mockups for “What Did That Really Cost?” — same content and controls, no nested rounded cards. Frozen on €60,000 salary and a pint in a pub. Directions 01–04 are references; 05 merges the preferred motifs.

Shared rules. Newsreader + Space Mono, yellow #F4CA16 as the only accent, hairline rules instead of card stacks. Salary slider, six purchases, share, earnings bar, segment stats, state equivalents, and additional detail all stay. Calculations are static.
What to look for. How salary sits without a panel, how purchases are indexed, how big numbers interrupt the page, and whether the earnings bar feels like a chart or a diagram. Pick motifs to merge before touching the live React tool.
