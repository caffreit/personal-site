Blog page redesign

Vertical accordion directions

Replacing the two-column grid of rounded cards with full-width panels stacked newest to oldest, where one panel is open at a time.

Shared rules. No drop shadows, no gradient washes, square corners. Search and tags are a quiet inline row rather than a container card. Every panel's text layer stands on its own — use the Images: On/Off toggle at the top right of each mockup to see how it degrades without covers. Timing is the 0.7s cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1) already used by the photos page.
Two interaction modes. Above 860px wide, hovering a panel opens it. Below 860px the stack pins to the viewport and the open panel is driven purely by scroll position — the first one is open on load, and each one closes as the next expands under your thumb, with no tapping. The switch is keyed to viewport width, so you can see the phone behaviour just by narrowing your desktop window.

Cinematic Rolodex

Light

The merge of 01 and 02, and the current front-runner. Nothing is laid over the photograph. Closed, a panel is a ledger line on white — text centred against a hairline rule, with a 220px strip of the cover peeking in from the right. Opening one wipes that strip out to full bleed at full colour while the text settles into a paper caption card at the bottom left, capped with a yellow rule. Page, panel, and card are all the same white, so there are no seams or false edges. You keep the big image and you keep clean type; they just stop competing for the same pixels.

Mood Editorial, gallery wall
Image treatment Untouched, no overlay
Legibility Guaranteed by the paper block
Trade-off Caption covers part of the photo
