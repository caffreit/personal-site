Prints Page Redesign

Five Directions

Each mockup presents a distinct approach to the prints buying experience — from gallery minimalism to cinematic immersion. Click any card to open its interactive prototype.

Direction 01
Gallery Wall
Minimalist fine art presentation. Single-column scroll, floating images with exhibition labels, maximum white space. Like walking through a gallery.
Minimalist White Space Single Column Light Theme
Direction 02
Editorial Spread
Magazine-feature treatment. Full-bleed heroes, dramatic typography, storytelling-first. Each print is a feature article. Buying feels like collecting.
Editorial Storytelling Typography Full-Bleed
Direction 03
Boutique Product
Premium e-commerce patterns. Product grid with hover quick-view, full configurator with size/paper selectors, room previews, trust accordions. Conversion-optimized.
E-Commerce Configurator Room Preview Conversion
Direction 04
Collector's Archive
Data-driven catalog. Table/grid toggle, catalog numbers, edition progress bars, specification tables, museum acquisition record detail pages. Swiss design meets database.
Data-Driven Catalog Table View Swiss Design
Direction 05
Immersive Showcase
Cinematic full-screen scroll. Each print takes over the viewport with Ken Burns animation. Glass-morphism detail drawer, glowing edition rings, dark atmospheric theme. Every print feels like an event.
Cinematic Dark Theme Full-Screen Immersive
Each mockup is fully interactive — size selectors, paper pickers, room previews, and navigation all work.
